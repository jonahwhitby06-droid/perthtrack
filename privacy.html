Privacy

What we keep and why.

What we store

If you create an account: your email address, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, your preferences, watchlist, the firms where you've said you know someone, your application pipeline and notes, and your alerts. Without an account, all of that stays in your own browser and never reaches us.

What we use it for

Only to run your account and send the alert emails you set up. Your LinkedIn connections file, if you add one on the My network page, is read in your browser and stays on your device; it never reaches us. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use advertising trackers.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you logged in. Nothing else.

Your control

You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account and all of its data, from your account page at any time.

Careers-page checks

Our nightly check reads firms' public careers pages, identifies itself, and respects each site's robots.txt.
