How the scores work. No firm publishes how many people apply, so competition is an estimate on a 1–5 scale built from three things: the firm's brand pull with students (Goldman, UBS, Macquarie, the Big 4 and the big resources companies draw the most), the usual intake size (mid-tier firms and insurers take more people per applicant), and how popular the team is (M&A and corporate finance push it up; audit and tax push it down because intakes are large). Firms with no advertised program score one point lower, because few students know to approach them, but there may be no role until you ask. Fit is 5 for your first two interests, 4 for the rest you pick, 3 for closely related work, 2 for other work in the same field and 1 for teams in a field you didn't pick. Score = weighting × fit + (1 − weighting) × low competition, out of 100. Use it to shortlist, then check each careers page.
