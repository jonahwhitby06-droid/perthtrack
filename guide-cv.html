Finance CV checklist

A checklist for a Perth finance internship CV, from the details recruiters check first.

Recruiters screening vacation applications spend very little time on each CV. Make the important things impossible to miss.

The basics

  • Your name at the top of the page body, not only in the header, because some application systems strip headers.
  • Your degree, majors, university and expected graduation month and year.
  • Your WAM or GPA if it helps you. A blank or missing mark reads as a low one.
  • One page for vacation programs; two at most for graduate roles.
  • Dates that make sense: no future end dates written as if they're done, and consistent formatting (e.g. “Nov 2023 – Present”).

Experience

  • Start each bullet with what you did, then the result: “Built a cash-flow model for a $40m infrastructure bid” beats “Responsible for modelling”.
  • Use numbers wherever you honestly can: dollar values, volumes, time saved.
  • Part-time retail or hospitality counts. Show responsibility (cash handling, training others, rostering) and customer work.
  • Remove every placeholder like “[X]” or “[type of project]” before you send it.

Skills

  • Name Excel specifically, and what you do in it (modelling, lookups, pivot tables).
  • List any valuation, accounting or data tools you've actually used.
  • Add relevant courses, case competitions and student society roles.

Final check

  • Save as PDF with a clear file name: Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf.
  • Read it aloud once. It catches more errors than spellcheck.
  • Ask someone in the industry to look at it. Your LinkedIn connections are a good place to start.
