All questions
Is PerthTrack free?

Yes. Tracking, alerts, the calendar and the application pipeline are all free.

Where do the dates come from?

Each program shows the dates its last cycle opened and closed, taken from the firm's own careers page or its listing on a graduate jobs board, with a link to the source. Most Perth firms reuse the same window each year, so the next window is projected from that and marked “est.” until the firm confirms it.

How do you know when a program opens?

Every night at 2am Perth time we read each firm's public careers page. If the page changes, or starts saying applications are open, it shows up in the live feed and in the alert emails of anyone following that firm.

What does “Apply any time” mean?

The firm has no advertised program, but hires students when the right person approaches them. These are often Perth boutiques. A short, specific email to someone in the team usually works better than the generic inbox.
