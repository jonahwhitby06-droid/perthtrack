How to connect your LinkedIn

Step-by-step instructions for adding your LinkedIn connections to PerthTrack, and using them to get a referral.

Connecting LinkedIn shows you, on every firm and team, which of your connections already work there. That's the fastest route to a coffee chat or a referral. Your connections are read in your browser and stay on your device.

Option 1

Paste your connections

About 30 seconds
  1. Click Connect LinkedIn at the top of any page.
  2. Click Open my LinkedIn connections ↗. Your LinkedIn connections page opens in a new tab. Sign in to LinkedIn if it asks.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of that page until all your connections have loaded. LinkedIn loads them as you scroll.
  4. Select and copy everything: ⌘ Cmd+A then ⌘ Cmd+C on a Mac, or Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C on Windows.
  5. Come back to PerthTrack, paste into the box (⌘ Cmd+V or Ctrl+V) and click Connect.

The firm is worked out from each person's LinkedIn headline, so people whose headline doesn't name their employer won't be matched.

Option 2

Upload LinkedIn's download

Most accurate · about 10 minutes
  1. Click Connect LinkedIn, then choose Upload LinkedIn's download.
  2. Click Open LinkedIn's data download ↗. This is in LinkedIn under Me → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  3. Choose “Want something in particular?”, tick only Connections, and click Request archive. LinkedIn may ask for your password.
  4. Check your email. LinkedIn sends a download link, usually within about 10 minutes. Download the zip file.
  5. Drop the zip file into the box on PerthTrack. There's no need to unzip it.

This includes each person's actual company and job title, so it matches more people and more teams.

Using it to get a referral

  1. Start with Best bets. Turn on “Only firms where I know someone” to see teams that fit you and where you have a way in.
  2. Message the person in the team you want. Keep it short: who you are, which program you're applying for, and one specific question about their team.
  3. Ask for 15 minutes, not a job. If the chat goes well, ask whether they'd be comfortable flagging your application.
  4. For 2nd-degree connections, use the “2nd-degree on LinkedIn” link on each firm, then ask your mutual connection for an introduction.
