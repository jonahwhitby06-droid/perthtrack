Law clerkships and paralegal roles: dates by state

How law clerkships work in each state, the shared dates for Perth, Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Adelaide, and how to find paralegal work while you study.

Clerkship dates by state (2026 round)

Each state's law society (or, in South Australia, the law students' societies) sets shared application and offer dates. Firms apply them office by office, so a firm can be open in Sydney and closed in Melbourne. Apply to the office where you want to start your career.

NSW also runs an open-market graduate round (22 Feb – 4 Apr 2027) and Queensland one in August. In Victoria and WA, most graduate roles go to former clerks first.

Perth in detail

Perth law recruiting is simpler than finance or engineering: most firms open and close clerkship applications on the same days, set by the Law Society of WA. Miss that window and most summer and winter clerkships are gone for the year.

The WA clerkship dates

For 2026, the Law Society of WA's dates were: applications open Monday 22 June, close Sunday 26 July, interviews from 27 July to 4 September, and offers made at 9am on Friday 11 September, held open until 1pm the same day. Expect a similar window in late June to late July 2027. The tracker marks these as estimates until the 2027 dates are published.

Clerkships are usually 3–4 weeks, in summer (November to February) and sometimes winter (June–July). They're mostly for penultimate-year students, and they're the main way firms choose their graduates.

Offer day

Offers come out on one morning and must be accepted or declined within a few hours. Decide your ranking of firms before offer day so you're not choosing under pressure.

Government and community legal

The State Solicitor's Office and the Office of the DPP run paid vacation clerkships, and in 2026 both closed in early August. The State Solicitor's Office selects on academic results and interest in government law, without interviews. The Ombudsman WA runs a summer clerkship, and Legal Aid WA recruits graduates from April.

Paralegal roles during semester

Paralegal and law clerk roles are the best way to get legal experience before or alongside a clerkship. A few firms run structured programs: K&L Gates' Perth casual paralegal program recruits final-year students in January and February, and Gilbert + Tobin keeps a rolling expression of interest for 3rd to 5th-year students. Most other roles are advertised one at a time, so set a SEEK or LinkedIn alert for "paralegal Perth" and check the WA Jobs Board for government roles.
