Finance cover letter template

A short, structured cover letter template for Perth finance internships.

Keep it under a page. The goal is to show you know what the team does and why you'd be useful in it, not to repeat your CV.

Template

Dear [Name, or the team's hiring manager], I'm a [year]-year [degree, major] student at [university], applying for the [program name] in your [team] team in Perth. Why this team: [One or two sentences about specific work the team does — a recent deal, a sector focus, a type of engagement — and why it interests you. Be specific to this firm.] Why me: [Your most relevant experience, with one concrete result. E.g. "During my internship at [firm] I built [model/analysis] for [purpose], which [result]."] [A second point: a skill, course or project that matches the work.] [If true: "I've spoken with [name] in your team, who described [something specific]."] I'm available from [date] for the full program. Thank you for considering my application. Kind regards, [Full name] [Phone] · [Email] · [LinkedIn]

Tips

  • Change the “why this team” paragraph for every firm. It's the part recruiters read most closely.
  • Only mention a contact if they've agreed to it.
  • Match the program name and team exactly as the firm writes them.
