The Perth finance recruiting timeline

When each kind of Perth finance program opens, month by month, and what to do before each window.

Perth recruiting runs on two clocks. Accounting and advisory firms mostly recruit early in the year; banks, investment banks and corporates mostly recruit mid-year. Missing the first clock is the most common mistake Perth students make.

February to April: the accounting and advisory wave

Most Big 4 and mid-tier vacation programs for the following summer open in February and March, and many winter programs open in the same window. Several close within a few weeks, and some assess applications as they arrive. Graduate programs for final-year students at firms like Findex/Crowe and Hatch also open around March.

Before February: finish your CV, get your transcript, and write one strong cover letter you can adapt.

May to August: banks, investment banks and corporates

Summer internships at the banks, the investment banks with Perth desks and the large resources and energy companies tend to open between June and August, alongside many graduate programs and the WA government finance agencies.

Before June: prepare for online tests and video interviews, which most of these programs use.

All year: boutiques and direct approaches

Many Perth corporate advisory boutiques, brokers and investment firms don't advertise. They hire when the right student gets in touch. These show as “Apply any time” in the tracker.
