How the score works

Six categories that always add up to 100. Every point given or taken away is explained with text from your CV.

CategoryPointsWhat we look at
Work experience and achievements25Action verbs, results and figures, duties versus accomplishments. In student mode, part-time work, projects, volunteering and clubs all count.
Writing quality and clarity20Vague or repeated wording, bullet length, grammar and spelling.
Structure and organisation15Contact details, education, experience and skills sections; order; length; bullet points.
ATS readability and compatibility15Standard headings, plain-text contact details, readable dates, signs of tables or columns.
Career relevance and targeting15A profile that names your target, specific skills, and skills backed up by your experience.
Professionalism and consistency10Consistent dates, bullets, tense and spelling; a professional email address.

We never invent facts

Suggested rewrites only rephrase what you wrote. Where a number would help, we ask you for it. Any rewrite that adds a figure that isn't in your CV is thrown out automatically.

Fair to students

Student mode doesn't mark you down for limited paid work. Age, gender, background and other personal characteristics are never scored.

Your CV stays private

Files are read in your browser. The rule-based check never sends your CV anywhere. AI analysis only runs if you choose it and tick the consent box. Privacy
