Winter internships in Perth

Where Perth students can find winter-break internships, and why they're worth chasing.

Winter programs are smaller than summer ones, but fewer students apply, and they often lead to summer or graduate offers. They're also one of the few options for final-year students who missed the summer round.

Who runs winter programs

Open the tracker filtered to winter for the current list. They fall into three groups: accounting and advisory firms with winter vacation intakes, consulting firms whose internships start in late June, and firms that take part-time or winter students on request.

When to apply

Most winter windows open in February and March, the same time as summer vacation programs at the same firms. If you wait until May to look for a winter role, most of these windows will have closed.

Final-year students

Many vacation programs are only for penultimate-year students. Use the “Open to final-year students” filter, and look at firms marked “Apply any time”: they have no year restriction, so a direct approach works.

The direct approach

For boutiques with no program, email a person in the team you want to join, not the generic inbox. Say which team, why that firm, what you'd do in the first week, and when you're available. Attach a one-page CV. Follow up once after a week.
