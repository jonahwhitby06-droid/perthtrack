Frequently asked questions

Is PerthTrack free?

Yes. Tracking, alerts, the calendar and the application pipeline are all free.

Where do the dates come from?

Each program shows the dates its last cycle opened and closed, taken from the firm's own careers page or its listing on a graduate jobs board, with a link to the source. Most Perth firms reuse the same window each year, so the next window is projected from that and marked “est.” until the firm confirms it.

How do you know when a program opens?

Every night at 2am Perth time we read each firm's public careers page. If the page changes, or starts saying applications are open, it shows up in the live feed and in the alert emails of anyone following that firm.

What does “Apply any time” mean?

The firm has no advertised program, but hires students when the right person approaches them. These are often Perth boutiques. A short, specific email to someone in the team usually works better than the generic inbox.

How are Best bets scored?

Each team gets a fit score (1–5) from how close its work is to the interests you pick, and a competition estimate (1–5). No firm publishes applicant numbers, so competition is an estimate from the firm's brand pull with students, typical intake size and how popular the team is. You choose how much each counts.

Which firms are covered?

About 1,000 programs across Perth, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Adelaide and Hobart. Finance: the Big 4, mid-tier and local accounting firms, corporate advisory boutiques, investment banks, trading firms, banks, insurers, super funds and fund managers, property firms, consultancies, regulators and state treasuries. Engineering: consultancies, contractors, mining, energy and process companies, utilities, transport agencies, defence and aerospace, medical device makers, surveyors and tech companies. Law: clerkships at top-tier, national and local firms; plaintiff and family law firms; government solicitors, DPPs, Legal Aid, Aboriginal legal services, public trustees, court associateships, regulators and paralegal roles. Choose a field, city and specialty in the bar under the menu.

Will I see every opportunity?

We list every program we know of with a regular intake, but smaller firms often advertise a single role on a job board instead. The Tracker's “Live listings” box opens the matching search on SEEK, LinkedIn, Indeed and SEEK Grad for your city, field and specialty, so check it weekly as well. If a firm is missing, email us and we'll add it.

Which cities are covered?

Perth, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Adelaide and Hobart. Choose a city (or All cities) from the menu at the top; your choice is remembered. National firms are listed once per city office, because most recruit per office and law firms follow each state's clerkship dates.

Can I filter by type of law or engineering?

Yes. The Specialty menu at the top lists every area: for engineering, civil, structural and transport, mechanical, electrical, chemical and process, mining, environmental, software, aerospace and defence, biomedical and project management; for law, corporate, banking, disputes, energy and projects, workplace, government, criminal and family, technology and IP, and paralegal roles. It shows only programs with a team in that area.

Will I miss roles that aren't programs?

Some firms advertise a one-off role instead of running a program. The tracker and home page link to live searches on SEEK, LinkedIn, Indeed and SEEK Grad for your city and specialty, so you can check those weekly. We link to the live search rather than copying listings.

Why do some dates say “typical timing”?

Where we couldn't confirm a firm's last dates, we show when that kind of program usually opens and label it unconfirmed. Treat it as a reminder to check the careers page, not a deadline.

Do you apply for me?

No. We link to the firm's own page. Always check details there before applying.

Is my data shared?

No. Your watchlist, applications and alerts are only used to run your account. You can export or delete everything from your account page.

How does Connect LinkedIn work?

Click Connect LinkedIn at the top, then either paste your LinkedIn connections page or drop LinkedIn's data download. It's read in your browser and stays on your device; it's never uploaded. Each firm then shows who you know there and which team they work in. LinkedIn doesn't share 2nd-degree connections, so each firm has a link to LinkedIn's own search for those.
