Frequently asked questions

Is PerthTrack free?

Yes. Tracking, alerts, the calendar and the application pipeline are all free.

Where do the dates come from?

Each program shows the dates its last cycle opened and closed, taken from the firm's own careers page or its listing on a graduate jobs board, with a link to the source. Most Perth firms reuse the same window each year, so the next window is projected from that and marked “est.” until the firm confirms it.

How do you know when a program opens?

Every night at 2am Perth time we read each firm's public careers page. If the page changes, or starts saying applications are open, it shows up in the live feed and in the alert emails of anyone following that firm.

What does “Apply any time” mean?

The firm has no advertised program, but hires students when the right person approaches them. These are often Perth boutiques. A short, specific email to someone in the team usually works better than the generic inbox.

How are Best bets scored?

Each team gets a fit score (1–5) from how close its work is to the interests you pick, and a competition estimate (1–5). No firm publishes applicant numbers, so competition is an estimate from the firm's brand pull with students, typical intake size and how popular the team is. You choose how much each counts.

Which firms are covered?

For finance: Big 4, mid-tier accounting, Perth corporate advisory boutiques and brokers, investment banks with Perth desks, the banks, insurers, super funds, property firms and WA government finance agencies. For engineering: the mining and energy companies, engineering consultancies, contractors, utilities and WA government agencies like Main Roads and the PTA. Use the switch at the top to show one or both. If a Perth firm is missing, email us and we'll add it.

Do you apply for me?

No. We link to the firm's own page. Always check details there before applying.

Is my data shared?

No. Your watchlist, applications and alerts are only used to run your account. You can export or delete everything from your account page.

How does Connect LinkedIn work?

Click Connect LinkedIn at the top, then either paste your LinkedIn connections page or drop LinkedIn's data download. It's read in your browser and stays on your device; it's never uploaded. Each firm then shows who you know there and which team they work in. LinkedIn doesn't share 2nd-degree connections, so each firm has a link to LinkedIn's own search for those.
