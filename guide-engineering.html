Engineering vacation programs in Perth

When Perth engineering vacation programs open, and how to plan around them.

Perth engineering recruiting is spread across the year, and the biggest employers recruit earliest. Students who start looking in September usually find most summer programs have already closed.

February to April: the big miners and government

BHP, Rio Tinto, Hancock and INPEX have run their summer intern rounds between late February and mid-April in recent cycles. Main Roads WA's cadetship and graduate program and Alcoa's graduate program also open in March. BHP's technology internship, which includes a winter intake for electrical, mechatronics and automation students, closed in late February.

Before February: finish your CV and get your transcript ready. Know which discipline and site types (Perth office, residential site, FIFO) you'd accept.

June to August: consultancies, utilities and energy

The Public Transport Authority's round is only about two weeks long, in late June. Water Corporation, Alcoa's vacation program, DOF and Chevron also recruit in June and July. Engineering consultancies such as GHD, Aurecon, Arup, AECOM and Lycopodium, along with Woodside, Newmont, Gold Fields and Thiess, mostly close in August.

September: the last few

Northern Star's summer roles stayed open until the end of September in the last cycle, and Civmec's graduate program closed in mid-September.

Winter options

Winter engineering internships are rare. In recent cycles they came from BHP's technology program and Thiess's winter vacation program. Smaller consultancies and contractors also take students on request, so a direct approach works.
